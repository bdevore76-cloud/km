Motion is what you see. Force is what makes it.

Kinetic Materials builds training aids that teach golfers the forces behind the swing, so they become better dynamic systems, not better poses.

Kinematics describes.
Kinetics explains.

Kinematics is the geometry of movement: positions, angles, speeds, sequences. It is what a camera captures and what most golf instruction talks about.

Kinetics is the physics underneath: the forces and torques that produce that motion. Two golfers can pass through the same positions and get there by completely different forces.

Kinematics

What did the body do?

Joint angles, club path, timing. Easy to see, easy to copy, and easy to mistake for the cause.

Kinetics

What made it do that?

Ground reaction, pressure, torque, and the way force moves through a body. Harder to see, and where the swing is actually made.

We take a kinetic-first approach. Everything we make has to help the golfer feel, sense and shape force, because better positions follow from a better system.
